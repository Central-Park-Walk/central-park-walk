## 1. Habit
- **One-liner:** *a small, broad, spreading tree — wider than tall — with **horizontal,
  layered, tiered branching** carrying a flat-ish spreading crown; graceful and open.*
- **Form:** broad, rounded, **wider than tall**, horizontally layered. **Aspect:** ~1–1.3 : 1
  (9–12 m spread on 9–15 m). **Fork:** low (`branch_start` 0.18), spreads early. **Branch
  character:** **horizontal, tiered, layered** (the cherry signature); somewhat zigzag spur
  shoots. **Asymmetry:** moderate; graceful lean.

## 2. Interaction
- **Stand:** layered spreading crowns **overlap horizontally into a soft tiered canopy**; in
  the Cherry Hill / Reservoir groves the bloom merges into a continuous pink-white cloud.
  **Target reading:** a low, layered, dappled grove — and in spring a merged blossom cloud.

## 3. Density
- **Bucket:** dappled (open-moderate). **Real:** LAI **2.5–4.0**; transmission ~20–35%.

## 4. Detail
- **Bark:** **smooth reddish-brown with prominent horizontal lenticel bands** (diagnostic).
- **Leaf:** alternate, simple, ovate, serrate, 6–15 cm; bronze-tinged at flush. **Summer:**
  medium green. **Fall:** yellow → bronze/orange, early. **Bloom — THE feature:** **profuse
  pale-pink-to-white blossom in early spring, before/with the leaves** (the park's signature
  display); fleeting.

## 5. Behavior
- **Wind:** light, moderate — open layered crown sways gently; petals flurry at bloom.
- **Season:** **blossom (early spring, pre-leaf)** → bronze flush → green summer → yellow-bronze early fall → bare tiered horizontal skeleton + banded bark (good winter read).

## 6. The one unmistakable thing
**Horizontal tiered branching + smooth banded reddish bark, erupting in a pink-white blossom
cloud in spring.** If it reads as an upright dense ball, it's wrong.

## 7. Variation envelope
- **Varies:** crown width/flatness, tier spacing, height (DBH), lean, bloom shade (pink↔white), bloom/fall timing.
- **Count:** **6–8** (high census; confirm picker >5).

## 8. Build mapping
- **Params** (`cherry` @ ln 586): keep low `branch_start` 0.18, **horizontal tiered** branches
  (flatness/gravity), open `leaf_density`; widen seeds to 6–8.
- **Textures:** banded reddish bark; ovate serrate leaf; **pink-white bloom + bronze flush** in seasonal recolor.
- **Placement:** horizontal overlap → tiered grove. **Perf:** open small tree; fragment-bound. Gate ×5.
