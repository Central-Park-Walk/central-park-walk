## 1. Habit
- **One-liner:** *a small, **often multi-stemmed**, broad spreading tree — wider than tall —
  forking very low, with smooth gray limbs carrying a rounded low crown; sculptural when bare.*
- **Form:** rounded, spreading, **wider than tall**. **Aspect:** ~1 : 1 (6–9 m spread on
  6–9 m). **Fork:** **very low** (`branch_start` 0.13), often multi-stemmed from near the base.
  **Branch character:** smooth, stout, low and spreading; upturned tips. **Asymmetry:** moderate; characterful low limbs.

## 2. Interaction
- **Stand:** low spreading crowns **overlap into a low mounded mass**; in bloom a merged
  pink-white cloud at eye level. **Target reading:** a low, sculptural, spring-flowering grove.

## 3. Density
- **Bucket:** moderate. **Real:** LAI **3.5–5.0**; transmission ~10–20%.

## 4. Detail
- **Bark:** **smooth light gray** (a feature — sculptural bare structure). **Leaf:** alternate,
  simple, **large obovate**, 8–20 cm, thick. **Summer:** medium-dark green. **Fall:** yellow-brown,
  undistinguished. **Bloom — THE feature:** **large pink-and-white tulip/saucer cups, BEFORE the
  leaves, very early spring** (the spectacle; fleeting, frost-sensitive).

## 5. Behavior
- **Wind:** stiff, low — heavy limbs and big leaves move little; petals drop quickly in wind.
- **Season:** **big saucer bloom (very early spring, pre-leaf)** → large-leaf summer → yellow-brown fall → **smooth-gray multi-stem sculptural skeleton with fuzzy flower buds** (winter).

## 6. The one unmistakable thing
**Large pink-white saucer flowers on a low, multi-stemmed, smooth-gray bare frame in very early
spring.** If it reads as a tall single-trunk tree, it's wrong — it's low, branchy, sculptural.

## 7. Variation envelope
- **Varies:** stem count (single ↔ multi), crown width, height, lean, bloom shade, bloom timing.
- **Count:** 5–6 (high census but `_s`-only; confirm picker).

## 8. Build mapping
- **Params** (`magnolia` @ ln 1011): keep very low `branch_start` 0.13 + multi-stem/low fork;
  spreading low crown. `_s`-only — don't generate m/l.
- **Textures:** smooth-gray bark; large obovate leaf; **pink-white saucer bloom + fuzzy buds** in seasonal recolor.
- **Placement:** low overlap → mounded grove. **Perf:** small; fragment-bound. Gate ×5.
