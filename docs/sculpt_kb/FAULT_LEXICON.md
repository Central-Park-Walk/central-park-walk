# FAULT LEXICON — botany sculpting

Confirmed, named faults. Every entry cites who named it, when, on what item. **Grows only
by key-author (Chris) confirmation** — a model naming a fault adds nothing until he rules.
This file is KB content regardless of any model's exam score (DESIGN.md: salvage either
way), and it doubles as the distractor/option pool for exam T2 items.

## Instrument faults (pipeline artifacts — not the sculpt)
- **FL-I1 overlay misregistration** — silhouette composited at wrong scale/aspect vs the
  locked ref; spills past the photo frame. Ground truth is geometric, arguable by no one.
  (Chris, 2026-08-25, item #0)
- **FL-I2 silhouette speckle** — thin tip geometry rasterizes to disconnected dots,
  unreadable as branches. (Chris, 2026-08-25, item #0)
- **FL-I3 coverage inversion** — composite covers regions where the photo shows no tree
  while missing the real crown. (Chris, 2026-08-25, item #0)

## Habit faults (the sculpt itself)
- **FL-H1 naked central mast** — a single unbranched leader runs to the apex; a real
  mature London plane forks into several steeply ascending leaders at ~¼ height.
  (Claude named · Chris confirmed, 2026-08-25, item #0)
- **FL-H2 menorah limbs + streamer tips** — limbs leave near-horizontal and hook upward
  at the ends; tips droop as wispy streamers; the reference shows stiff, ascending,
  fine upper-crown branching. (Claude named · Chris confirmed, 2026-08-25, item #0)

## Foliage / render faults (pre-existing, from LEDGER ## 47)
- **FL-F1 distance card confetti** — foliage cards dissolve into confetti at range.
  (Chris, LEDGER 47)
- **FL-F2 leaf-shell-hugs-wood** — leaf shells shrink-wrap the branch geometry instead of
  occupying crown volume. (Chris, LEDGER 47)
