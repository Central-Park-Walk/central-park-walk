## Detailed Findings

### 1. Bethesda Fountain — Angel of the Waters

**Professional scan exists but is NOT publicly downloadable.**

Direct Dimensions (hired by Central Park Conservancy) performed a full 3D laser scan of the 40-foot fountain using Artec Eva and Artec Ray scanners. High-resolution point clouds were created for preservation documentation. Only fragments are shared publicly:

- **Bethesda Fountain Face (Test)**
  - URL: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/bethesda-fountain-face-test-754b31c9548349e7be3f9f0d17d26056
  - Creator: Direct Dimensions (@dirdim)
  - Polygons: 233.8K tris / 117.6K verts
  - License: NoAI (not downloadable)
  - Format: View-only on Sketchfab

- **Bethesda Fountain Angel Hand**
  - URL: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/bethesda-fountain-angel-hand-4755c00f2ad44702a4c0bfab2eb9709c
  - Creator: Direct Dimensions (@dirdim)
  - Polygons: 999.6K tris / 500.1K verts
  - License: NoAI (not downloadable)

- **Bethesda New York Park** (third-party designed model, NOT a scan)
  - URL: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/bethesda-new-york-park-f6566e5f2f6c4768a1142e071d7344b2
  - Creator: astistudio
  - Polygons: 920.2K tris / 498.5K verts
  - License: Sketchfab Store (paid, royalty-free)
  - Note: This is an artistic model, not a photogrammetry scan

- **Bethesda Fountain Central Park NY** (designed model, NOT a scan)
  - URL: https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/architecture/bethesda-fountain-central-park-ny
  - Creator: Ociam
  - Price: $10 USD
  - Format: OBJ, STL
  - License: Cults PU (proprietary, NoAI)
  - Note: 3D-print-ready design, no textures
